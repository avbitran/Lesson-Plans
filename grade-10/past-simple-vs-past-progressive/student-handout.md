## Part A: Read

### The Night the Lights Went Out

Last winter, I was studying for a maths test at my friend Noa's apartment. It was raining heavily and the wind was blowing hard. Noa's little brother was watching cartoons in the living room, and her parents were cooking dinner. Suddenly, all the lights went out. Noa screamed, the TV stopped, and the whole building went silent.

While Noa was looking for a flashlight, I opened the window. Our neighbours were standing on their balconies and shouting. Then I noticed something strange: one apartment across the street still had light. A man was sitting at his window and playing the guitar. We didn't know why his electricity was working. Later, we found out that he had a generator.

When the electricity came back an hour later, we weren't studying anymore. We were singing along with him. I didn't do well on the test, but I remember that night better than anything I learned for it.

## Part E: Alibi

> Yesterday between 7:00 and 9:00 p.m., someone ate the entire birthday cake in the teachers' room. You are all suspects!

**Suspects:** with your partner, agree on what you were doing together between 7:00 and 9:00. Remember the details. You can't write anything down!

**Detectives:** question each suspect alone. Write down any answers that don't match.

**Question bank for detectives**

- Where were you at 7:00?
- What were you doing at 7:30? At 8:15? At 8:45?
- What was your friend wearing?
- What was the weather like?
- What did you eat? Who paid?
- Did anything interrupt you? What happened?
- What were the people around you doing?
- When did you leave? How did you get home?
