D

Write it yourself

10 points

D1 · 2 × 2 points

  1. My brother was sleeping when the fire alarm went off.
    or: The fire alarm went off while my brother was sleeping.
  2. While I was doing my homework, my parents were watching the news.
    Both verbs progressive: two long actions at the same time. "When I was doing…, my parents were watching…" is acceptable.

2 points: both tenses right. 1 point: one verb wrong, or a form error (e.g. "was go off"). 0: both wrong.

CriterionPoints

Model answer: At 7:30 my mum was making coffee and my sister was looking for her shoes. My dad was reading the news on his phone. While I was eating breakfast, the cat jumped on the table and knocked over my juice.

Reading the results

Low marks in A and B mean the student still mixes up the forms: re-teach with the timeline and the colour-coded story. A good A–B with a weak C usually means questions and stative verbs need another round. Part D separates students who can use the tenses from those who can only recognise them.
